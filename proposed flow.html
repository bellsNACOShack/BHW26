1. You find placement
2. ⁠You get a letter of acceptance from said placement
3. ⁠You upload that letter on bell’s ITCU portal
4. ⁠You download, print and fill a form from the ITCU portal called a SCAF form. (You will submit it physically to the nearest ITF office)
5. ⁠You fill your logbook, write comments, date it, sign it, give it to your supervisor, he does the same thing (plus stamping)
6. ⁠ After completing your SIWES (meaning your logbook is filled), you go to ITF to get it signed.
7. ⁠After that, you take it to school for submission.
8. ⁠The school grades your SIWES report, defense AND logbook to give you a gpa for second semester.
